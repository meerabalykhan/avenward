# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Avenward a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Use welcoming language, respect differing viewpoints, accept constructive
feedback, focus on what is best for the community, and show empathy.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, unwanted sexual attention,
doxxing, publishing private information, and conduct inappropriate in a
professional setting are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Maintainers may
remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban
contributors whose behavior is inappropriate.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
